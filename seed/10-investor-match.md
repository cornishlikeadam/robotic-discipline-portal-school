## 3. Do Not Email List (Wrong Stage, Wrong Sector, or Dead Path)

* **Do NOT email Series A+ growth funds** (Sequoia Growth, Tiger Global, General Atlantic) — you have `$0` revenue (`00-context.md`).
* **Do NOT email B2B SaaS-only funds** (Bessemer Enterprise Cloud, SaaStr Fund) — they do not fund 3D-printed consumer animals.
* **Do NOT pay "pitch-to-investors" events or cold-DM crypto "launchpads"** — anyone asking a founder for a fee to pitch or list a token is a predator.
