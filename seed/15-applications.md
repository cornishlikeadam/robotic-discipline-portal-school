## 1. Universal Checklist for Every Application Answer

Run every answer against these five boxes before pasting into an application form:
- [ ] **A specific person, not "users":** Names a 20-year-old SCAD maker or collector, not "consumers globally."
- [ ] **A fact or a labeled gap:** Links a public fact or says `"0 paid users today; testing with 3 adults this month."`
- [ ] **One sentence a stranger can repeat:** The first sentence stands alone without jargon.
- [ ] **No slogan doing the work of a noun:** Zero banned words (`leverage`, `unlock`, `robust`, `seamless`, `game-changer`).
- [ ] **Under the word limit before you add color:** Cut adjectives first.

## 2. The 11 Common Application Prompts (Bad vs. Better + Coach Questions)

### 1. Problem
* **Bad example:** "In today's digital landscape, users suffer from fragmented AI experiences and static collectibles that fail to unlock persistent utility."
* **Better example (Animal Scrypt):** "People paying $9.99/month for Character.ai or $19.99/month for Replika complain every week on Reddit that their bots forget project details and persona facts after a few dozen messages (https://www.reddit.com/r/CharacterAI/). Meanwhile, buyers spent $5.1 billion on Pop Mart figures in 2025 for plastic characters that sit on a desk and do nothing (https://www.popmart.com). Makers and students have no way to keep an AI worker's memory tied to a physical object they own."
* **If you are stuck, answer these:** Who did you watch get frustrated by an AI forgetting something this week? What exact task were they doing? How many minutes did they waste repeating themselves?

### 2. Solution
* **Bad example:** "Animal Scrypt is a next-gen physical-digital ecosystem leveraging AI agents and blockchain royalties to empower creators."
* **Better example (Animal Scrypt):** "Animal Scrypt (built by Robotic Discipline) pairs a 3D-printed animal shell with a browser workspace that remembers facts you explicitly click Approve to save. Each animal takes a worker role—engineer, artist, musician, or researcher—and recalls your approved project details across weeks, even after a server restart. When you train an animal on your workflow and someone forks a child animal from your template, our ledger credits you a 20% royalty."
* **If you are stuck, answer these:** What does the buyer hold in their hand? What happens on their screen 5 seconds after they scan the QR/NFC tag? What is the one thing the animal still knows 7 days later?

### 3. Team
* **Bad example:** "I am a passionate visionary founder with a diverse background in leadership and technology."
* **Better example (Animal Scrypt):** "I am Kendren 'KJ' Cornish, a rising sophomore at SCAD in Savannah, Georgia. I served about eight months in the U.S. military on a diver path, work as a resident assistant on campus, and help run my family's nonprofit and church conference. At SCAD, I have direct access to 3D-printing labs to fabricate shells and also work on a SCADpro research thread applying AI sensing to horse gait and vocalization."
* **If you are stuck, answer these:** What is the hardest physical or technical thing you have built or finished under a deadline? What campus tools can you walk into today for free that a normal founder has to rent?

### 4. Traction
* **Bad example:** "We have massive early momentum, a growing pipeline of 300 founding members, and multiple strategic partnerships in motion."
* **Better example (Animal Scrypt):** "We have 0 paid users and $0 in revenue today. What exists today is our working local Python and SQLite state kernel (`skeleton/interfaces.py`) that enforces user-approved memory (`PROPOSED -> APPROVED -> DELETED`), portable JSON identity export, and simulated royalty records. Our next 6 weeks are locked to 7 checkboxes: printing 1 shell with a QR/NFC tag, proving 7-day recall of 3 approved details across restarts, and running 3 consenting adults at SCAD through activation and Day-7 return use."
* **If you are stuck, answer these:** Which part of the code runs on your laptop right now without crashing? How many real people have touched it? What is the exact date you will hand the first shell to Tester #1?

### 5. Business Model
* **Bad example:** "We monetize through a multi-tiered Web3 tokenomics flywheel, subscriptions, and enterprise B2B licensing."
* **Better example (Animal Scrypt):** "We sell the physical 3D-printed animal shell + Month 1 workspace starting at $45 (`assumption: ~$10.61` unit cost), followed by a $9/month memory subscription. As we prove 7-day retention and add worker roles, our prices step up to $129–$249 for multi-part pro docks and guided learning kits, plus $19–$49/month subscriptions. When a user pays to fork a Child Animal from a trained Parent Animal, the parent's maker receives a 20% royalty."
* **If you are stuck, answer these:** What does the very first buyer pay you on Day 1? How much does the plastic, tag, and API call cost you out of pocket? Which single event triggers a royalty payout?

### 6. Target Customer
* **Bad example:** "Anyone who uses AI, loves collectibles, has a disability, or wants to educate their kids."
* **Better example (Animal Scrypt):** "Our first customer is a tech-curious young adult (18–30), starting with the 18,550 art, design, and engineering students at SCAD who already use AI for multi-week studio projects and collect desk figures (https://www.scad.edu/about/scad-glance). After we prove 7-day recall with adult makers, we expand into higher-tier kits for people with disabilities seeking accessible daily memory companions and parents wanting Alpha School-style self-directed learning tools (https://alpha.school)."
* **If you are stuck, answer these:** Name three real people in your dorm or studio building who have both a figure on their desk and ChatGPT open on their laptop. Why would they pay $45 this Friday?

### 7. Competitors
* **Bad example:** "We have no direct competitors because no one else combines AI, 3D printing, and Litecoin."
* **Better example (Animal Scrypt):** "We compete against chat apps like Character.ai ($9.99/mo) and Replika ($19.99/mo), desktop robots like Living.AI EMO ($279; https://living.ai), and NFC figure makers like Tonies (EUR 630M FY25 revenue; https://ir.tonies.com). Chat apps lose context across sessions; EMO dies in 2 hours and fails on dorm Wi-Fi; Tonies only plays static audio. We lose today on brand awareness and polished mobile apps, but we win on a $7.50 (`assumption:`) battery-free shell linked to an inspectable memory ledger and creator royalty split."
* **If you are stuck, answer these:** What is the customer using today instead of your animal? Why haven't they switched yet? Where would Character.ai or Pop Mart beat you today?

### 8. Advantage / Unfair Edge
* **Bad example:** "Our proprietary AI orchestration engine and first-mover advantage."
* **Better example (Animal Scrypt):** "Three things work together here that copycats rarely build at once: (1) an explicit user-approved memory gate so the animal never stores chat hallucinations, (2) art-school fabrication at SCAD where we print and film shells on campus without factory minimums, and (3) a parent-child royalty ledger that pays users for training a useful worker animal."
* **If you are stuck, answer these:** Why can you print and test 10 physical shells in Savannah faster and cheaper than two software engineers in a San Francisco apartment?

### 9. Timing / Why Now
* **Bad example:** "AI is booming and everyone wants personalized agents."
* **Better example (Animal Scrypt):** "Three curves crossed in the last 18 months: small/fast LLM inference dropped below $1.50/user/month (`assumption:`), adult collectible demand hit $5.1B+ at Pop Mart alone in 2025 (https://www.popmart.com), and early NFT creature failures like CryptoZoo taught buyers to demand working software and physical utility before touching digital provenance (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CryptoZoo)."
* **If you are stuck, answer these:** Why would this have been too expensive or impossible for a college sophomore to build three years ago?

### 10. Acquisition / Distribution
* **Bad example:** "We will grow via viral social media loops, SEO, and influencer partnerships."
* **Better example (Animal Scrypt):** "Our first 50 users come from three zero-ad channels: (1) in-person studio and dorm demos at SCAD in Savannah, (2) raw 30-second videos of each shell coming off the 3D printer and passing its Day-7 memory check, and (3) *The Book of Robotic Discipline*—our free serialized build essays and startup worksheets that convert readers into beta testers."
* **If you are stuck, answer these:** How will you get Testers #1, #2, and #3 without spending $1 on ads? What makes Owner #1 show the shell to their roommate?

### 11. Milestones (Next 6–8 Weeks vs. 6 Months)
* **Bad example:** "Launch v1, grow to 10,000 users, and launch our marketplace."
* **Better example (Animal Scrypt):** "Next 6–8 weeks: finish the 7-checkbox MVP (1 printed QR/NFC shell, 1 persistent personality, 7-day recall of 3 approved facts across restarts, 1 browser workspace, 1 ownership view, 1 simulated royalty transfer, and 3 consenting adult tests). Next 6 months (Accelerator window): ship 50 paid shells at $45–$129 (`assumption:`), publish our dated case study and methods white paper, and move the royalty ledger from test mode to live payouts."
* **If you are stuck, answer these:** What will be true on Friday of Week 6 that is not true today?

## 3. Program-Specific Notes

| Program | What They Punish | What to Emphasize for Animal Scrypt | What NOT to Do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SCAD Accelerator** | Treating it like a class project, missing twice-weekly sessions, or pitching a small local shop (`accelerator_1_transcript.pdf` p. 11–13). | You are combining SCAD physical craft (3D-printed shells) with a scalable software/royalty platform, and pairing it with SCADpro. | Submitting two separate applications or hiding that you are pre-revenue. |
| **Y Combinator** | Vague team, fake traction, or ideas with zero user contact. | How fast you can ship the 7-day memory demo and print shells on campus. Lead with user-owned memory ("Personal AGI"). | Leading with a Litecoin/crypto thesis. YC funds the animal if the animal is the company. |
| **Techstars** | Being uncoachable or not knowing what you need from mentors. | Name the exact 90-day mentor gap: scaling shell fabrication beyond campus printers and converting the first 50 paid users. | Applying to an enterprise B2B city track with no consumer/hardware mentors. |
| **a16z Speedrun** | Consumer products that sound like homework or lack a daily return loop. | The collectible + worker loop: you come back on Day 7 because the animal remembers you and evolves its role. Show the zine UI and shell. | Using B2B SaaS or enterprise agent jargon. |
