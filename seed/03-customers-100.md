## Group 1: SCAD Students Who Already Make Objects (Seats 1–30 of 100)
*(Includes neurodivergent students and students with disabilities who want an accessible, low-friction memory and study assistant)*

* `simulation:` **Job to be done:** Keep track of a 10-week studio capstone, CAD measurements, or research references without digging through 40 lost ChatGPT tabs at 2 a.m.
* `simulation:` **The objection:** "Why do I need a 3D-printed figure when I already pay $20/month for ChatGPT Plus or use free Claude?"
* `simulation:` **Price they laugh at:** $2,500 (they are college students buying filament and coffee).
* `simulation:` **Price they might try:** $25–$45 one-time for a custom shell + $0–$9/month if they can run it on their laptop or campus Wi-Fi.
* `simulation:` **Sentence that makes them stop and look:** "Tap this figure on your phone, tell it three facts about your studio build today, and next Friday it still knows your exact dimensions without mixing up your other classes."

## Group 2: Collectors Who Buy Figures and Also Use AI Tools (Seats 31–60 of 100)
*(Ages 18–30 who buy Pop Mart, Bandai/Gundam, MechatroWeGo, or Amiibo)*

* `simulation:` **Job to be done:** Own a numbered, physical desk creature that actually has a personality, remembers its owner, and can be traded or forked with provenance.
* `simulation:` **The objection:** "Is this another dead NFT rug-pull like CryptoZoo where the toy is cheap plastic and the app shuts down in six months?"
* `simulation:` **Price they laugh at:** $500+ for an unproven brand with no artist pedigree.
* `simulation:` **Price they might try:** $49–$89 for a numbered, zine-packaged 3D-printed figure that works offline/locally even if our servers go down.
* `simulation:` **Sentence that makes them stop and look:** "Every figure has its own memory ledger that you can export as a file, and if someone forks the worker role you trained, the ledger credits your parent figure."

## Group 3: Parents or Older Teens (16+) Buying Homework-Adjacent Project Help (Seats 61–85 of 100)
*(Inspired by Alpha School's 2-hour self-directed learning model, scoped strictly to parents and older teens—no under-13 child-directed data collection in v1)*

* `simulation:` **Job to be done:** Help a teen stay on track with a self-paced coding, art, or study block without the parent nagging them or paying $40,000/year for private AI school tuition (https://alpha.school).
* `simulation:` **The objection:** "I don't want my teenager talking to an unmoderated chatbot like Character.ai, and I don't understand crypto wallets."
* `simulation:` **Price they laugh at:** $2,500 upfront for a prototype with no curriculum track record.
* `simulation:` **Price they might try:** $59 starter kit (figure + parent-visible memory dashboard) + $12/month.
* `simulation:` **Sentence that makes them stop and look:** "The animal never saves a memory unless your teen clicks approve on the dashboard, so you can see the exact study goals it remembers every week."

## Group 4: Small Creative Studios Renting a Worker-Animal for a Week (Seats 86–100 of 100)
*(2–5 person design, animation, or indie game teams)*

* `simulation:` **Job to be done:** Borrow a pre-trained researcher, archivist, or style-checker agent for a 1-week sprint without spending 10 hours configuring Relevance AI workflows (https://relevanceai.com).
* `simulation:` **The objection:** "A plastic toy on a desk doesn't help my team ship client work unless the output is better than our normal prompts."
* `simulation:` **Price they laugh at:** $2,500 for a collectible unless it saves 20 hours of billable work.
* `simulation:` **Price they might try:** $29–$49 for a 1-week worker lease or $99/month studio seat.
* `simulation:` **Sentence that makes them stop and look:** "Another SCAD designer already fed this archivist creature 50 material specs; you can rent its worker role for one week without seeing their private notes, and they get a cut."

## Reality Check: The Only Real Next Step Is 10 Conversations

Simulated buyers do not pay bills. To satisfy MVP done-condition #7 in `00-context.md` (*"At least three consenting adults independently complete activation, conversation, and return-use tasks"*), you need **10 real conversations** this week at SCAD (Group 1 + Group 2) to pick the first 3 adult testers.

### Mom Test Questions to Ask This Week (Ask About Their Past Week, Never Pitch Your Idea First)
1. "Show me the figures or objects sitting on your desk right now. Which was the last one you bought, how much did it cost, and what have you done with it since unboxing?"
2. "Pull up your AI chat history from this week. When did the AI forget something you already told it, and what did you do to fix it?"
3. "Tell me about the last project or study block where you got stuck working alone. How did you keep track of where you left off between Monday and Friday?"
4. "Have you ever shared a custom prompt, GPT, or workflow with a friend? Did anyone pay you for it, or did they just copy-paste it?"
5. *(Only at the end, holding the 3D-printed shell):* "I am testing three 3D-printed animals this week that remember three approved facts about your studio project for seven days. Can we scan this on your phone right now and set a 5-minute check-in for next Sunday?"
