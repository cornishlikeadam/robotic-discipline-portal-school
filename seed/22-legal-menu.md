## 1. Four Corporate Approaches Compared in Plain English

| Approach | What it is | Why it might fit | Why it might not | Do not do this yet |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Single-member or multi-member LLC** (e.g., Georgia or Wyoming LLC) | Default U.S. small company with pass-through taxes and low annual fees | Simple, cheap (`~$100` state filing), one product, one founder selling `$45` shells on campus | Awkward if you take institutional VC or issue standard YC corporate SAFEs (`19-term-sheet.md`) | Do not promise "shares" or sign a Delaware C-Corp SAFE from an LLC with no conversion paperwork |
| **C-Corp (usually Delaware)** (or Delaware PBC) | Standard corporate box that issues Common and Preferred Stock | What YC (`$500k` deal; https://www.ycombinator.com/deal), Techstars, and a16z Speedrun expect on Day 1 | Franchise taxes, separate corporate tax return, and board minutes | Do not form a C-Corp and ignore annual Delaware franchise tax notices |
| **Wyoming DAO / DUNA** (Decentralized Unincorporated Nonprofit Association) | Chain-native legal wrapper for member-governed token protocols | Only fits if governance and treasury are actually run by on-chain token votes and a lawyer confirms DUNA compliance | Easy to cosplay. Animal Scrypt v1 is a one-founder product company, not a decentralized protocol state | Do not put customer money or founder IP into a DAO because the brand likes the word |
| **Holding company / conglomerate** (Parent + Trust + Subsidiary LLCs + Foundation) | Parent company owns separate IP company, product company, and studio | Useful years later if two operating businesses have totally different liability or investors | Premature. Three working names (`Animal Scrypt`, `Disciple`, `Madken`) are three marks, not three companies | Do not build a 4-entity conglomerate chart for a pre-revenue MVP (`$0` sales) |

## 2. Recommendation (Founder May Override)

**Use ONE legal entity for the MVP: `Robotic Discipline, Inc.` (or `Robotic Discipline LLC`).**

* **How to choose between an LLC now vs. a Delaware C-Corp now:**
  1. If you are taking outside accelerator money in the next 90 days (YC, Techstars, a16z Speedrun, Dorm Room Fund), ask a startup lawyer about forming **one Delaware C-Corporation** (`Robotic Discipline, Inc.`) using a standard founder package (e.g., Clerky or Cooley GO; https://www.cooleygo.com) so your cap table and 83(b) election are clean.
  2. If you are bootstrapping on campus at SCAD for the next 6 months with zero outside investors, a **single-member LLC** (`Robotic Discipline LLC`) gives you a liability shield and a bank account for ~$100, which a lawyer can convert to a Delaware C-Corp when an accelerator accepts you.
* **Written "Not Now" Parking Lot:**
  * **Wyoming DUNA / DAO:** Parked until **Condition A** is met—at least 100 independent external creators are earning recurring parent-child royalties and require decentralized protocol governance reviewed by crypto securities counsel.
  * **Holding Company / Conglomerate:** Parked until **Condition B** is met—a second operating business (such as the equine telehealth project or a physical fabrication shop) generates independent revenue and requires a separate liability firewall.

## 3. IP Questions to Take to a Lawyer (Questions, Not Legal Opinions)

Bring these five questions to a licensed attorney and to the SCAD Accelerator director before selling commercial batches:

1. **Chain of Title (Founder to Company):** "How do I execute a clean Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement transferring my existing code (`skeleton/`), prompts, character lore, and the names *Robotic Discipline*, *Animal Scrypt*, *Madken*, and *Disciple* into the single company?"
2. **SCAD Student & Lab IP Policy:** "Under SCAD's current student intellectual property policy, if I use campus 3D printers, SCADpro studio time, or the SCAD Accelerator to prototype Animal Scrypt shells, does the university claim any ownership or license over my character designs or code?"
3. **Collaborator & Tester Releases:** "What 1-page Work-for-Hire / IP Assignment do I need if a SCAD classmate helps me sculpt a 3D shell, and what 1-page Informed Consent / Release do I need for the 3 consenting adult MVP testers (`00-context.md` checkbox #7)?"
4. **Reference Art Clearance:** "Our early workspace used a photo of a third-party Japanese robot toy (`madken1.jpeg`) as a moodboard reference. How do we confirm all public pages, shells, and decks use 100% original sculpted geometry so we never infringe third-party trade dress?"
5. **What the Litecoin / Ownership Record Transfers:** "In our Terms of Service, how do we state clearly that buying a physical shell and an ownership record transfers ownership of that specific physical object and a license to use its workspace—**not** the underlying copyright or trademark of the species/brand?"
