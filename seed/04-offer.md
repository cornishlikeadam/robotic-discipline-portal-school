## The V1 Offer

### 1. Who It Is For
Tech-curious young adults (ages 18–30, starting with SCAD students, makers, and figure collectors in Savannah) who build projects across multiple days and want a physical desk companion that remembers their context.

### 2. What Result They Get
1. **One physical creature:** A numbered, 3D-printed shell with a QR/NFC tag sitting on their desk.
2. **One persistent worker-animal:** A named digital character with a biography and browser workspace that stores only the project or study facts the user explicitly approves.
3. **Seven-day verified recall:** When the user comes back seven days later—even after a server or laptop restart—the animal accurately recalls at least three approved details from Week 1 and uses them in its worker role (engineer, artist, musician, or researcher).
4. **One ownership and royalty record:** A verified owner page where spawning a child worker from their animal records a creator royalty allocation in the ledger.

### 3. In What Time
* **Activation to first approved memory:** Under 5 minutes from scanning the shell's QR/NFC tag.
* **Core proof window:** 7 days from Day 1 memory approval to Day 7 recall check (`00-context.md` done-condition #3).
* **Full MVP delivery window:** 6–8 weeks (`00-context.md`).

### 4. What They Give Us
* **In the 3-Adult MVP Proof (Weeks 1–6):** 15 minutes on Day 1, one mid-week check-in, 15 minutes on Day 7, and permission to record what worked and what broke for our case study (`28-case-study.md`).
* **In the Paid Pilot (Weeks 7–8):** `assumption:` **$39–$49 one-time** for the 3D-printed shell + **$9/month** after the first 30 days if they keep using the memory workspace.

### 5. What We Refuse to Promise
* We do **not** promise passive crypto income or rising token floor prices.
* We do **not** promise medical, clinical, or therapeutic treatment for loneliness or disability.
* We do **not** promise that the animal remembers everything ever typed; it remembers **only** the facts the owner clicks **Approve** to keep.
* We do **not** promise a walking, motorized robot in v1; the v1 shell is a 3D-printed physical twin with a passive QR/NFC tag.
