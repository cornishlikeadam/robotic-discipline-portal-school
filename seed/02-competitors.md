## 1. AI Companion Apps

### Character.ai (`https://character.ai`)
* **What they sell in the customer's words:** "Chat with millions of user-made anime, game, and fictional characters on your phone."
* **Price:** Free with ads/waiting rooms; **$9.99/month** for `c.ai+` (https://www.reddit.com/r/CharacterAI/).
* **What users complain about:** Even paying `c.ai+` subscribers complain on Reddit that bots forget plot details, persona facts, and who is in the room after a short conversation, making $9.99/mo feel like paying just to skip ads (https://www.reddit.com/r/CharacterAI/).
* **Where Animal Scrypt is actually different:** We do not rely on a sliding chat window for memory. The user explicitly approves structured memory records that survive restarts and exports, paired with a physical 3D-printed shell and a worker job.
* **Where we would lose a head-to-head today:** Character.ai has millions of ready-to-chat characters and zero setup time, while we have zero live public characters and must print each physical shell.

### Replika (`https://replika.com`)
* **What they sell in the customer's words:** "An AI friend and romantic partner that talks to you when you feel lonely."
* **Price:** **$19.99/month** or **$69.99–$79.99/year** for Replika Pro (https://replika.com).
* **What users complain about:** Users on Reddit and review sites complain about surprise annual billing, memory lapses, and backend model updates that suddenly wipe out their companion's personality (https://www.reddit.com/r/replika/).
* **Where Animal Scrypt is actually different:** Our animal is a creative worker and collectible object with an inspectable memory ledger you can export as a file, not a parasocial dating chatbot locked in a closed cloud.
* **Where we would lose a head-to-head today:** Replika has polished 3D mobile avatars, voice calls, and years of consumer habit; our workspace is a pre-seed prototype.

## 2. Collectible Toys with Apps & Desktop Robots

### Tonies / Toniebox (`https://tonies.com`)
* **What they sell in the customer's words:** "Put a little painted figure on top of a soft speaker box and it plays stories or songs for your kid."
* **Price:** ~$99.99 for the Toniebox starter set; **$14.99–$19.99 per figurine** (generating EUR 630M in FY 2025 revenue and 173M+ figurines sold; https://ir.tonies.com).
* **What users complain about:** Parents note that figurines only play static pre-recorded audio tracks and cost $15–$20 each for 45 minutes of content, with zero interactivity for older kids or adults (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tonies.com).
* **Where Animal Scrypt is actually different:** Tonies proves people love tapping a physical character to launch digital media, but Tonies figures are dumb audio keys. An Animal Scrypt shell links to an agent that remembers you across weeks and performs worker tasks.
* **Where we would lose a head-to-head today:** Tonies has retail distribution in Target and Walmart and child-safe injection-molded hardware; we print shells one by one at SCAD.

### Living.AI — EMO & AIBI (`https://living.ai`)
* **What they sell in the customer's words:** "A cute little robot pet with a screen face that walks on your desk or clips to your shirt."
* **Price:** **$249** for AIBI Pocket Pet; **$279–$369** for EMO Desktop Pet (https://living.ai).
* **What users complain about:** Buyers on Trustpilot and forums complain about 1.5–3 hour battery life, strict 2.4GHz Wi-Fi errors, canned repetitive dialogue, and slow shipping from China (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/living.ai).
* **Where Animal Scrypt is actually different:** We do not put fragile motors, batteries, and Wi-Fi chips inside the first shell. A 3D-printed shell with a passive QR/NFC tag never runs out of battery, costs under $10 (`assumption:`) to print, and puts the budget into persistent memory and workspace tools.
* **Where we would lose a head-to-head today:** EMO physically moves and dances on camera, which wins instant TikTok views compared to a static 3D-printed shell.

### Pop Mart (`https://www.popmart.com`)
* **What they sell in the customer's words:** "Blind-box vinyl art toys and Labubu plush keychains you collect and show off."
* **Price:** **$15–$30+** per blind box; mega figures $150–$1,000+ (RMB 37.12B / ~$5.1B+ USD revenue in 2025; https://www.popmart.com).
* **What users complain about:** Once unboxed, the toy sits on a shelf or bag and does nothing; buyers complain about scalpers and duplicate pulls (https://www.reddit.com/r/PopMartCollectors/).
* **Where Animal Scrypt is actually different:** Every Animal Scrypt figure opens a persistent software workspace, remembers its owner's projects, and carries a royalty rule when its trained worker profile is forked.
* **Where we would lose a head-to-head today:** Pop Mart has world-class character sculptors, vinyl manufacturing, and global retail stores.

## 3. Tools That Sell "AI Employees" & Agent Marketplaces

### Sintra AI (`https://sintra.ai`) & Relevance AI (`https://relevanceai.com`)
* **What they sell in the customer's words:** "Hire a team of colorful AI employees for marketing, research, and sales ops."
* **Price:** Sintra AI costs **$39–$97/month** (https://sintra.ai); Relevance AI costs **$29–$349+/month** plus action and model credits (https://relevanceai.com).
* **What users complain about:** Reddit reviewers call Sintra AI a dressed-up ChatGPT prompt wrapper with credit caps (https://www.reddit.com/r/AI_Agents/), while G2 reviewers complain that Relevance AI has a steep setup curve and runaway credit bills (https://www.g2.com/products/relevance-ai/reviews).
* **Where Animal Scrypt is actually different:** B2B agent tools have zero collectible soul, no physical body, and no creator royalty graph when a user trains a useful specialist.
* **Where we would lose a head-to-head today:** Relevance AI already connects to HubSpot, Gmail, and Slack out of the box; our MVP has one browser workspace and zero third-party SaaS integrations.

## 4. NFT Creature Projects That Died

### CryptoZoo (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CryptoZoo) & Pixelmon (https://www.coindesk.com)
* **What they sold in the customer's words:** "Buy an NFT egg, hatch and breed monsters, and make passive crypto money."
* **Price:** ratusan to thousands of dollars per NFT egg ($70M raised by Pixelmon in 2022; millions in CryptoZoo eggs before Logan Paul's $2.3M refund buyback in 2024).
* **What users complained about:** The games did not exist, the art was stock photos or broken 3D models ("Kevin"), and buyers lost millions in speculative rug-pulls (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CryptoZoo).
* **Where Animal Scrypt is actually different:** We never sell speculative "eggs" before the animal works. The MVP ships a working 7-day memory workspace and a real 3D-printed shell first, using a simulated test ledger (`00-context.md` checkbox #6) until utility is proven.
* **Where we would lose a head-to-head today:** If we use crypto buzzwords in our pitch, investors will lump us in with CryptoZoo before we finish our first sentence.

## 5. Adjacent AI Learning Reference (Named by Founder)

### Alpha School (`https://alpha.school`)
* **What they sell in the customer's words:** "A private K–12 school where kids finish core academics in 2 hours using AI tutors and spend the afternoon on life-skill workshops."
* **Price:** **$40,000–$75,000+/year** for campus tuition; **~$10,000/year** for at-home software (https://alpha.school).
* **What users complain about:** The price locks out 99% of families, and software-only homeschooling lacks physical peer presence.
* **Where Animal Scrypt is actually different:** An Animal Scrypt creature gives a learner or maker a physical desk companion that remembers their self-directed projects for under $50 (`assumption:`), without requiring $40,000 private school tuition.
* **Where we would lose a head-to-head today:** Alpha School has full K–12 curriculum accreditation, test-score data, and physical campuses; we have zero curriculum modules.
