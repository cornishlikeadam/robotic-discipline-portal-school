## 1. Bottom-Up First (People We Can Actually Reach)

### Step A — Reachable Campus Beachhead (SCAD Makers)
* **Total SCAD student count:** **18,550 students** (15,552 undergrads and 2,998 grad students across Savannah, Atlanta, and SCADnow; https://www.scad.edu/about/scad-glance).
* **Harsh conversion `assumption:`** Suppose only **1.5%** of SCAD students try a paid Animal Scrypt shell in Year 1 (`18,550 × 1.5% = ~278 buyers`).
* **Year-1 Price Mix (`assumption:` from `05-pricing.md`):**
  * First 50 buyers pay `$45` for the v1 shell + 6 months of `$9/mo` memory (`$99` first-year value) = **`$4,950`**.
  * Next 228 buyers pay the Tier 2 price (`$129` shell + 6 months of `$19/mo` = `$243` first-year value) = **`$55,404`**.
  * **Reachable SCAD-Only Year-1 Revenue (`assumption:`):** **`~$60,354`** from one campus with zero paid ads.

### Step B — Reachable Art/Design Students + Independent Figure Collectors (US Beachhead SOM)
* **Count:** Suppose we reach **50,000** U.S. design/engineering makers and adult mecha/vinyl figure collectors via campus demos, print videos, and *The Book of Robotic Discipline* in Years 1–2 (`assumption:`).
* **Harsh conversion `assumption:`** **3% conversion** (`1,500 active owners`).
* **Blended annual spend as prices grow (`assumption:` from `05-pricing.md`):**
  * Average `$149` hardware kit + `$19/month × 12` memory workspace + 1 forked child worker (`$30` net royalty/platform cut) = **`$407 / owner / year`**.
  * **Reachable Bottom-Up SOM (1,500 owners):** **`1,500 × $407 = $610,500 / year`** (plus any `$2,500` Founding Cohort passes sold after proof).
  * At **2,500 owners** (`5%` of 50,000), annual revenue crosses **`$1,017,500 / year`**.

## 2. Top-Down Second (Cited Public Reports & Why to Distrust Them)

### Serviceable Available Market (SAM) — Interactive & Collectible Figures
* **Tonies SE (Physical NFC Figurines + Audio Box):** Generated **EUR 630 million (`~$660M–$680M USD`)** in FY 2025 revenue, with **EUR 447 million** coming strictly from figurines and **173 million+ figurines** sold cumulatively by mid-2026 (https://ir.tonies.com).
* **Pop Mart (Adult Collectible Character Figures):** Generated **RMB 37.12 billion (`~$5.1B+ USD`)** in 2025 revenue, up 184.7% year-over-year (https://www.popmart.com).
* *Why these two numbers matter:* They prove consumers already spend over **`$5.7 billion/year`** across just two companies for physical characters and NFC-paired figurines.

### Total Addressable Market (TAM) — Global AI Companion Market
* **Grand View Research & Precedence Research:** Value the global AI Companion market at **`$36.8 billion to $37.12 billion` in 2025** and **`~$48.0 billion to $48.63 billion` in 2026**, projecting 31% annual growth through 2033–2035 (https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/ai-companion-market-report).
* **Why that top-down report is the wrong market for us:** That `$48B` report lumps together enterprise customer-support chatbots, voice assistants like Siri/Alexa, and clinical mental-health software—none of which sell a 3D-printed collectible animal with a creator royalty graph.
