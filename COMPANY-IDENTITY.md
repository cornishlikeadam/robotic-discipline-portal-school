## 1. Mission Statement

**Build a physical-digital AI companion ecosystem where collectible characters act as recognizable, user-controlled interfaces to useful digital intelligence, creative workspaces, and low-pressure connected experiences.**

---

## 2. Core Philosophy

Modern AI is trapped behind two broken interfaces:
1. **Disembodied cloud chat boxes** that flatten every tool, project, and personality into an infinite scrolling text thread with opaque cloud memory.
2. **Parasocial companion apps or gimmicky AI gadgets** that either isolate users in synthetic echo chambers or fail because they attempt to replace the smartphone with expensive proprietary hardware.

**Robotic Discipline** proposes a third path rooted in human-computer interaction and historical craft:
* **Tangible Anchor, Modular Mind:** A physical figure on your desk gives intelligence a recognizable face, role, and boundary. Placing an **Engineer** figure, an **Athlete** figure, or a **Scholar** figure onto a dock switches your cognitive and digital context immediately—opening the right workspace, tools, files, and memory scope.
* **User-Owned Continuity:** Intelligence does not require moving giant model weights onto a plastic toy. What travels with the character—via NFC pairing, a removable cartridge, or a signed JSON bundle—is **identity, authored lore, user-approved memories, credentials, and explicit permissions**.
* **Discipline Before Autonomy:** In keeping with *The Book of Robotic Discipline* ("Make the machine obey exactly what you mean" and "Set the rules before you hand over power"), no character silently records personal facts, shares private data, or executes consequential actions without explicit human approval.

## 4. Corporate & Legal Structure Analysis

Earlier notes proposed a three-part hybrid structure:
1. **Delaware Public Benefit Corporation (PBC)** as the commercial operating company.
2. **Founder's Trust** holding majority voting shares (60%–80%+).
3. **Wyoming DUNA (Decentralized Unincorporated Nonprofit Association)** or nonprofit DAO layer for open memory standards, community treasury, and inclusion grants.

### Disciplined Legal Recommendation for Stage 1
* **Do NOT incorporate multiple entities or launch a token/DAO before customer traction.** Forming a PBC, a Trust, and a Wyoming DUNA simultaneously creates thousands of dollars in legal/accounting overhead and severe investor due-diligence friction at the pre-seed/accelerator stage.
* **Start with ONE clean operating entity** (either a standard **Delaware C-Corporation** or **Delaware Public Benefit Corporation (PBC)** if applying to venture accelerators like YC/Techstars/a16z Speedrun, or a **single-member LLC** converted to a Delaware C-Corp/PBC upon institutional funding) holding 100% of the IP for Robotic Discipline, MADKEN, Animal Scrypt, and Disciple.
* Keep the **Wyoming DUNA / cooperative layer** on the long-term roadmap (Phase 6/7) only after open protocol governance or community-owned infrastructure is operationally justified.
