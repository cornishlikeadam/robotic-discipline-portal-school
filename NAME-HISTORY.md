# NAME HISTORY & CONCEPT GENEALOGY

**Purpose:** Document how **MADKEN** (*Mad Kin*), **Animal Scrypt**, **Disciple**, **Space Fort**, and **Robotic Discipline** evolved as expressions of the same founding vision by Kendren "KJ" Cornish, ensuring no concept is silently lost or mistakenly treated as a separate company.

---

## 1. Chronological & Conceptual Genealogy

### A. Animal Scrypt (`Untitled document 2026-11-06 20.56.13.md`, `Madken space fort 2.pdf`, `DUNA_MAdken.pdf`)
* **Core Focus:** Persistent, privacy-first AI companions with evolving personalities, user-approved memory ledgers, and low-pressure social connection.
* **Signature Contributions to the Unified Vision:**
  * **Low-Pressure Social Bridge:** Solving everyday loneliness without high-friction social performance through **Parallel Presence** (doing activities at the same time in silent co-working habitats), **Shared Ritual Objects** (digital or 3D-printed artifacts that evolve when two or more people check in together and gently rest rather than punish missed days), and **Bilateral Memory Fragment Exchanges** (both humans must explicitly approve sharing a short continuity note).
  * **Local-First Agent OS (`DUNA_MAdken.pdf`):** Using local runtimes (Ollama + LangGraph + SQLite checkpointer + long-term memory store + router pattern) so personal data remains on user-controlled hardware.
  * **Specialized Community & Homestead Roles:** Expanding from individual companions into family memory keepers, neighborhood mutual-aid stewards, and **Capability Flash-Leases** (borrowing a companion's worker role—archivist, researcher, reminder, property steward—via a temporary, auto-expiring permission token without exposing private memories).
  * **Science & Longevity Expansion Arm (Stages 0–4):** Long-term reinvestment thesis where consumer companion revenue funds neighborhood robotic/wet-lab co-ops, a sustainable data center, and eventual orbital/space research twins.

### B. MADKEN / Mad Kin (`More_madken_university.pdf`, `DUNA_MAdken.pdf`, `Madken space fort 2.pdf`, `madken1.jpeg`)
* **Core Focus:** Physical collectible action figures as tangible interfaces to a StarNetOS/Spaceport offline computing workspace and an interconnected fictional civilization.
* **Signature Contributions to the Unified Vision:**
  * **The Three Connected Identities of a Figure:**
    1. *The Collectible:* Physical design, rarity, faction, biography, provenance/NFT certificate, and ownership history.
    2. *The Living Character:* Personality, memories, relationships, profession, and role inside the simulated MADKEN civilization.
    3. *The Operating Agent:* Offline AI capabilities, specialized tools, connected device controls, and multi-agent collaboration.
  * **Archetypal Functional Classes (`More_madken_university.pdf`):**
    * **Engineer** (*Fictional role:* Builds machines → *Real-world utility:* Robotics, CAD workflows, repair manuals, diagnostics).
    * **Commander** (*Fictional role:* Leads a faction → *Real-world utility:* Coordinates multi-agent teams).
    * **Athlete** (*Fictional role:* Trains competitors → *Real-world utility:* Workout tracking, training log review, sports drills, movement analysis).
    * **Architect** (*Fictional role:* Designs settlements → *Real-world utility:* Building local digital twins of apartments, homes, or facilities).
    * **Scholar** (*Fictional role:* Preserves knowledge → *Real-world utility:* Research, knowledge libraries, and education).
    * **Guardian** (*Fictional role:* Protects infrastructure → *Real-world utility:* Smart-home and environmental sensor monitoring).
  * **The Desk Dock & Multi-Agent Council:** Placing one figure on a desktop dock recognizes its NFC identity and opens its workspace (*Spaceport*). Placing 2–3 figures (e.g., Researcher + Designer + Engineer) on a shared dock lets them collaborate on a real-world challenge (like designing a small robot) while their fictional personas negotiate treaties, trade resources, or compete inside the civilization engine.
  * **The Transferable Intelligence Cartridge:** A removable hardware/digital cartridge that separates a character's continuity (identity, settings, approved memories) from its plastic shell, allowing it to move between a figurine, a desktop dock, or a larger robot without unauthorized duplication.
  * **6-Year Release Cadence:** Starting with 12 founding characters across 4 factions, releasing new waves every 6 months across 4 repeating capability phases toward ~1,100 agent skills.

### C. Space Fort / ORBIT / Action Intelligence (`Madken space fort 2.pdf`, `PROJECT_BRIEF.md`)
* **Core Focus:** The institutional umbrella and technical interoperability standards (*The Artificial Civilization Project*).
* **Signature Contributions to the Unified Vision:**
  * **Portable Identity Profile (`ORBIT`):** Separating portable identity, relevant memories, credentials, and permissions from heavy model weights so a character can move across computers, figures, and heterogeneous robots.
  * **StarNet / Survival LLM:** Offline-first, message-driven computing (including SMS/iMessage-style interfaces and local HTML worlds) that operates without constant cloud dependency.
  * **ArtClass & Campus/Dormitory Pilots:** Helping students study, coordinate research teams, and represent community spaces.

### D. Disciple & Robotic Discipline (`The Book of Robotic Discipline Book Plan.pdf`, Skill/Data Room PDF, Master Prompt v2.0)
* **Core Focus:** The unified company identity (**Robotic Discipline**), the founder's self-Reliant AI engineering and business operating system (**Disciple**), and the 18-Law field manual (*The Book of Robotic Discipline*).
* **Signature Contributions to the Unified Vision:**
  * **Founder Operating System (`gstack`-inspired virtual team):** 17 specialized AI roles (CEO, PM, Customer Researcher, CFO, CTO, Robotics Engineer, QA, Release Manager, etc.) sharing a single verified company seed context and enforcing strict Test-Driven Development (TDD).
  * **The Book & Company Flywheel:** Writing *The Book of Robotic Discipline* (18 Laws in 4 parts + 10 business worksheets modeled on historical inventors like Vaucanson, Jacquard, Babbage, and Lovelace) using real numbers and experiments from building the company, turning every chapter into proof of work and a customer acquisition channel.
  * **Investor Data Room & Open-Source VC/Startup Tool Directory:** Self-hosted, role-permissioned data room with Stage 1 / Stage 2 NDA gates, audit logs, multilingual support (EN/FR/DE), and a curated directory of open-source startup/VC tools.
