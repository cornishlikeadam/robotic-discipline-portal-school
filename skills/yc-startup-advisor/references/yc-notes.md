# Attributed YC Startup School Notes (`references/yc-notes.md`)

Public syllabus reference: https://www.startupschool.org

1. **What a Startup Is (Sam Altman & Dustin Moskovitz):** Hard, ambitious problems attract better people and less shallow copycat competition than easy derivative ideas. Growth and retention drive everything else.
2. **Startup Ideas (Paul Graham & Kevin Hale):** The best ideas often look like toys initially, grow from something the founder builds for themselves, and are evaluated by how cheaply and quickly you can test repeat usage with real people.
3. **Talking to Users & Product (Adora Cheung & Kevin Hale):** Watch users attempt your core task without guiding their mouse. Measure real behavior, not compliments.
4. **Monopoly & Secrets (Peter Thiel):** Start by owning a tiny, specific market completely (for Animal Scrypt: SCAD makers in Savannah) before expanding into concentric circles.
5. **Doing Things That Don't Scale (Walker Williams & Justin Kan):** Recruit your first 10–50 users manually in person and onboard them one by one.
6. **Growth & Retention (Alex Schultz):** Retention is the only real foundation for growth. Until Day-7 and Day-14 retention curves flatten above zero, do not spend money on ads or viral loops.
7. **How to Raise Money (Marc Andreessen, Ron Conway & Paul Graham):** Keep burn so low that you don't need outside money to survive the month (`~$200/mo` campus bootstrap burn in `06-cfo.md`). When raising pre-seed, use standard SAFEs (`19-term-sheet.md`), state gaps honestly, and close fast.
