## The three parts of a character

The new university notes distinguish:

1. **Collectible:** design, biography, faction, provenance, and possible physical or digital ownership record.
2. **Living character:** personality, fictional memories, relationships, missions, and civilization state.
3. **Operating agent:** permitted tools, relevant knowledge, user-approved memory, and practical capabilities.

These parts can interact without mixing authored fiction with facts about a person. A transferable identity can preserve selected information and permissions while using different models and hardware. This is a design direction, not an implemented interoperability standard.
